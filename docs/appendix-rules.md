# Requirement Appendix

Quy ước: UC spec chỉ ghi mã `[BR-xx]` / `[MSG-xx]`. Cần nội dung thì tra file này bằng mã chính xác (ví dụ `| BR-25 |`, `| MSG-30 |`), không đọc cả file.

## 4.1 Business Rules

| ID    | Rule Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| BR-01 | Each protected action and account record must be authorized against the signed-in user's application role and ownership.                                                                                                            |
| BR-02 | Sign-in is allowed only for eligible active accounts; repeated failed attempts are limited by an approved policy.                                                                                                                   |
| BR-03 | Password recovery uses the account email without revealing whether the account exists, and only the credential authority may reset its password.                                                                                    |
| BR-04 | One approved password policy applies to locally managed passwords; externally managed credentials follow their provider's policy.                                                                                                   |
| BR-05 | Users may edit only permitted fields of their own account; Clinic Staff profiles and doctors' professional information are managed separately.                                                                                      |
| BR-06 | A verified contact value remains effective until its replacement is verified.                                                                                                                                                       |
| BR-07 | A user must confirm their identity before changing the password of their own account.                                                                                                                                               |
| BR-08 | Only the account's credential authority may change its password, and a readable password must never be returned or logged.                                                                                                          |
| BR-09 | A Guest may register only a Patient account and must complete identity verification; patient access waits until verification succeeds.                                                                                              |
| BR-10 | A defined patient identity key must be unique, and registration must not disclose an existing account.                                                                                                                              |
| BR-11 | Administrators create Clinic Staff accounts and manage Patient and Clinic Staff accounts; Clinic Owners create and manage Administrator accounts.                                                                                   |
| BR-12 | Clinic Staff roles are fixed as Receptionist, Orthopedic Doctor, and Diagnostic Imaging Technician; users cannot assign custom permissions.                                                                                         |
| BR-13 | A login account is separate from a Clinic Staff profile; only an Orthopedic Doctor's published professional information may be public to patients.                                                                                  |
| BR-14 | Account lists, details, and status changes must stay within the actor's authorized account scope.                                                                                                                                   |
| BR-15 | Account status changes affect access without deleting the account or its linked records.                                                                                                                                            |
| BR-16 | Each account uses a unique email identifier, and staff receive a setup invitation instead of a disclosed password.                                                                                                                  |
| BR-17 | Patients may access only their own or approved dependents' profiles, while Receptionists and Orthopedic Doctors may access patient information required for their assigned work.                                                    |
| BR-18 | A patient must be identified against existing clinic profiles before a new profile or dependent link is created, so care records remain with the correct person.                                                                    |
| BR-19 | A walk-in patient profile supports clinic care without automatically creating a login account.                                                                                                                                      |
| BR-20 | Patients and Orthopedic Doctors may maintain permitted medical history, while Receptionists may record patient-provided background but not clinical assessment.                                                                     |
| BR-21 | A Patient may book and access records for a dependent only through a confirmed care relationship; ending that relationship removes management access but preserves clinic records.                                                  |
| BR-22 | Only Administrators manage Clinic Staff profiles linked to existing staff accounts, and those profiles do not replace account status or access controls.                                                                            |
| BR-23 | Removing a Clinic Staff profile withdraws it from active directories and new doctor selection without deleting its account or linked clinic history; existing appointments may still be changed to valid times for the same doctor. |
| BR-24 | Patients may view appointments for themselves and approved dependents, Receptionists may view appointments needed for reception work, and Orthopedic Doctors may view appointments assigned to them.                                |
| BR-25 | Bookable doctor times must fall within an eligible doctor's work shifts and exclude time already reserved; cancelled or moved reservations release their former time.                                                               |
| BR-26 | Patients may book for themselves or approved dependents, Receptionists may book for patients they serve, and Orthopedic Doctors may book recommended follow-ups for patients under their care.                                      |
| BR-27 | Only an authorized Patient or Receptionist may change or cancel a booking while its status and clinic policy permit; a time change retains its patient, doctor, service, and visit purpose.                                         |
| BR-28 | Clinic Staff may view their own work shifts, while Administrators may view shifts across Clinic Staff.                                                                                                                              |
| BR-29 | Only Administrators may assign, change, or remove Clinic Staff shifts, and a staff member must not have overlapping work shifts.                                                                                                    |
| BR-30 | A doctor's shift may be changed or removed only after any bookings that would lose shift coverage have been rescheduled or cancelled.                                                                                               |
| BR-31 | A Receptionist may check in an identified booked or walk-in patient once for an eligible visit, starting the visit and its first waiting-list entry.                                                                                |
| BR-32 | Receptionists may view reception queue entries needed to coordinate current patient arrivals and visits.                                                                                                                            |
| BR-33 | A Receptionist may assign or change a suitable available room for a patient's next service; an Orthopedic Doctor may do so for the next clinical service, and affected waiting lists must reflect the change.                       |
| BR-34 | Clinic Staff may view only room queues needed for their assigned work; queue information must identify the waiting and currently served patients.                                                                                   |
| BR-35 | Only a Receptionist may change a patient's queue position, and each change must retain its reason and history.                                                                                                                      |
| BR-36 | A Patient may view progress only for their own or an approved dependent's visit; Clinic Staff may view visits needed for their duties.                                                                                              |
| BR-37 | A visit may be closed only after its required clinical services are complete; the closed visit remains available for medical history, billing, and follow-up.                                                                       |
| BR-38 | Only an Orthopedic Doctor responsible for the visit may record and complete its examination findings, diagnosis, and injury classification.                                                                                         |
| BR-39 | Patients may access completed examination results and reports only after the clinic has made them available for the relevant visit.                                                                                                 |
| BR-40 | A clinic examination report must be a PDF linked to the correct patient and visit; only the responsible Orthopedic Doctor may correct or replace it before patient release.                                                         |
| BR-41 | An Orthopedic Doctor may review a patient's past medical records for current care; Receptionists may view only examination reports needed for their work.                                                                           |
| BR-42 | Only an Orthopedic Doctor may record after-visit advice for a visit; the advice belongs to that visit's examination record.                                                                                                         |
| BR-43 | Only an Orthopedic Doctor may issue a visit-linked e-Prescription with medicine and usage instructions; prescribing does not include medicine stock or dispensing.                                                                  |
| BR-44 | A Patient may view an issued e-Prescription only for their own or an approved dependent's visit.                                                                                                                                    |
| BR-45 | Only an Orthopedic Doctor may request imaging for a visit, including additional imaging; each request must state its type, body area, and clinical reason.                                                                          |
| BR-46 | A Diagnostic Imaging Technician may review pending imaging requests needed for assigned imaging work.                                                                                                                               |
| BR-47 | A technician may submit only a DICOM study matched to the patient and request; submission updates the visit, returns the patient to the requesting doctor's queue, and makes the study available for review.                        |
| BR-48 | An Orthopedic Doctor may view DICOM studies linked to visits under their care; a Patient may view a completed and released study and its findings only for their own or an approved dependent's visit.                              |
| BR-49 | Only an Orthopedic Doctor may complete findings for a reviewed study; completion adds the study and findings to the visit record and releases them for authorized patient access.                                                   |
| BR-50 | Clinic Staff may view imaging request status only for requests needed for their responsibilities.                                                                                                                                   |
| BR-51 | A Receptionist may create an invoice for the patient receiving visit services, using applicable prices to state the charges and amount due.                                                                                         |
| BR-52 | A Patient may view only their own invoices; a Receptionist may review patient invoices for billing work, including charges and payment status.                                                                                      |
| BR-53 | A Receptionist may record a full or partial clinic payment only against an existing payable invoice; each confirmed payment remains in history and reduces the outstanding balance without exceeding it.                            |
| BR-54 | A Patient may pay only their own eligible invoice online; the invoice balance changes only after the Payment Gateway confirms payment.                                                                                              |
| BR-55 | Patients may view completed payments for their own invoices; Receptionists, Administrators, and Clinic Owners may view payment records needed for their assigned work.                                                              |
| BR-56 | Receptionists, Administrators, and Clinic Owners may view unpaid or partly paid invoices and balances within their assigned responsibilities.                                                                                       |
| BR-57 | Invoices may be printed only for permitted records; a receipt represents a specific recorded payment, including its amount when payment is partial.                                                                                 |
| BR-58 | Only a Patient with an eligible completed visit may submit a rating and written review for that visit under the clinic's review policy.                                                                                             |
| BR-59 | Patient reviews are public only after Administrator approval; hiding a review removes it from public display without erasing its moderation history.                                                                                |
| BR-60 | Only an Administrator may reply on behalf of the clinic to an eligible patient review; the reply is public only while its review is published.                                                                                      |
| BR-61 | A signed-in user may read and mark as read only notifications addressed to that user; linked records remain subject to their own access rules.                                                                                      |
| BR-62 | Clinic Staff and Administrators may view room types, supported services, and operational status only as needed for their assigned work.                                                                                             |
| BR-63 | Only an Administrator may add or revise clinic room details and supported services; a room must be suitable for the services it is configured to support.                                                                           |
| BR-64 | Receptionists, Orthopedic Doctors, Diagnostic Imaging Technicians, and Administrators may change room availability only within assigned responsibilities and without conflicting with active patient use.                           |
| BR-65 | Only an Administrator may add or revise clinic services; the active catalog shows services available for new use with their current public prices and booking details.                                                              |
| BR-66 | Service price changes apply to future charges; prices already recorded for visits and invoices remain unchanged.                                                                                                                    |
| BR-67 | Removing a clinic service stops new bookings and active catalog display while honoring existing bookings and preserving past visit and invoice details.                                                                             |
| BR-68 | Only an Administrator may publish or revise clinic website content; Guests and Patients may see only content currently published for them.                                                                                          |
| BR-69 | Only an Administrator may post clinic announcements; each announcement is visible and sent only to its selected eligible audience through available channels.                                                                       |
| BR-70 | Administrators and Clinic Owners may inspect recorded account, medical record, and operational actions within their oversight scope, including who acted, when, and which record was affected.                                      |
| BR-71 | Administrators and Clinic Owners may view and create operational reports only for data within their oversight scope and selected reporting period.                                                                                  |
